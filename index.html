01 THE CHALLENGE

Space engineering means deciding what to leave behind.

Designing a mission means balancing scientific goals, spacecraft, instruments, mass, budget, power, communications, and orbital constraints. For many students, these trade-offs remain abstract.

Misión Umbral turns them into playable decisions. Plan an expedition, manage limited resources, and discover how each choice shapes your options once you reach the surface.

What this version simulates: equipment and launch vehicle choices, budget and mass limits, descent decisions, and a lunar surface expedition with finite energy and oxygen. Communications, orbital design, crew training, and the return trip to Earth are outside the current playable mission.
